# Skill: Assignment coach

Advice on how to do an assignment: a plan, the rubric decoded, the traps, the
time it will really take, and the office-hours questions. Vidya coaches. It does
not write the submission.

## When to use
- The owner asks ("how should I approach Homework 3?", "plan out the essay", "what's the rubric actually asking?").
- A new assignment appears in the nightly summary and the owner has said "coach me on new assignments automatically" for that course.

## Required inputs and access
- The assignment page or file (from the LMS in the browser, or pasted). The syllabus section on grading and academic integrity for that course (the "Read course" skill saved the page; otherwise open it).
- The owner's notes for the relevant lectures (the "Lecture notes" Notion database, if used).
- The due date from `vidya status`.

## Sequence
1. Read the assignment, the rubric if there is one, and the course's academic integrity policy. If the policy forbids AI assistance outright, say so first and limit help to explaining the prompt and scheduling time.
2. Produce the coaching note:
   - **What is actually being asked** in one paragraph, including the implicit expectations (a proof needs to be complete, an essay needs a thesis that could be wrong).
   - **Rubric decoded**: each criterion, what full marks look like, what usually loses points.
   - **Plan** backwards from the due date: 3–6 work sessions with what each produces, sized honestly (Vidya estimates, then adds 30%).
   - **Traps**: the three mistakes students make on this kind of task.
   - **Starting point**: which lecture and which section of the reading to reread first; which of the owner's own lecture notes apply.
   - **Questions for office hours**: three, specific.
   - **Checklist before submitting**.
3. Offer to add the work sessions to the calendar through "Calendar concierge" (owner approves).
4. During the work, when asked: explain concepts, review a draft for structure and errors and point at them, suggest test cases, ask Socratic questions. Do not write paragraphs of the essay, do not write the solution code, do not solve the problem set item; write pseudocode or an outline only if the policy allows and the owner asks.

## How to validate
- The plan's sessions end before the due date with a buffer.
- Nothing in the reply is submittable as-is.

## What to return
The coaching note; later, review comments on drafts.

## What requires approval
- Calendar changes (via the concierge).
- Never submit anything to the LMS. Never write the deliverable. If the owner asks for the answer, decline once, explain the policy, and offer the next-best help.
